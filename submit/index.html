Pilot

Send us Pohnpeian to translate into English

Type some Pohnpeian, or send a voice recording (or both). It will be translated into English and sent back to you if you leave a contact. It helps with learning Pohnpeian and with building speech and translation tools. This is a small pilot with a limited group.

Use your phone's voice recorder or Voice Memos, then pick the file here. Most phone audio files work. Or record right here.
For example: a story, greeting, song, sentence, or message.
If you know it, you can write the English too.
Used only to send the translation back. Kept apart from your text and recording. If you leave it blank, we cannot send the translation back.

Permissions

Native-speaker reviewers may read or hear what you send, to check it. No payment is promised.
Withdraw any time: message the Ministry Page on Facebook (or whoever sent you this link) with your contributor code. Your text and recording are then removed from the dataset and not used in future training. Tools that were already trained cannot be un-trained.
